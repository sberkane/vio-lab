READING THE EXPERIMENT

Model and measurements

Prescribed rigid-body motion is observed through spherical bearings and a fixed landmark set. The quadcopter marks the body pose; no motor or flight-controller model is simulated. All studies retain the observer’s rotational correction in the position equation.

01

Individual flow constraints

The ideal bearing–flow relation gives one homogeneous constraint on velocity, without feature depth or velocity-direction reconstruction. Noise perturbs the constraint itself, so its residual need not vanish at the true velocity.

Ideal model: Hi = si× πbᵢ · HivB = 0
02

Gravity guides attitude

The velocity–gravity subsystem evolves independently of the attitude correction. Estimated gravity then corrects tilt. The ideal-model guarantees concern body-frame velocity, gravity, and reduced attitude.

Visual residual → Riccati correction → tilt
